Guidelines 

## Brand & Style

The design system centers on a "Personal Organizer" aesthetic—moving away from sterile corporate SaaS environments toward something that feels like a well-maintained physical planner. The brand personality is clean, trustworthy, and highly organized, utilizing a white and purple foundation to convey academic reliability with a modern, approachable touch.

The visual style is **Corporate / Modern** but softened with organic whitespace and a "High-Utility" focus. It prioritizes clarity for scheduling and financial tracking, using a systematic approach to status colors to ensure the interface remains legible and actionable at a glance.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout follows a **Fluid Grid** philosophy optimized for mobile-first interactions. It relies on a consistent 4px/8px baseline shift to maintain rhythm.

- **Margins:** A standard 16px (1rem) safe area is maintained on the horizontal edges of the screen.
- **Structure:** Content is organized into clear vertical stacks. Large gaps are avoided in favor of subtle background shifts (`--bg-subtle`) to denote different functional areas.
- **Financial Views:** Use tabular layouts with IBM Plex Mono, ensuring vertical alignment of decimal points for professional-grade financial clarity.

## Elevation & Depth

This system utilizes **Tonal Layering** rather than heavy drop shadows to create a clean, modern aesthetic. 

- **Level 0 (Background):** Pure white or `--bg-subtle`.
- **Level 1 (Cards):** White surfaces with a very soft, low-opacity neutral shadow or a simple 1px border in `--purple-100` to define edges without adding visual weight.
- **High Elevation:** Reserved specifically for the central "Add (+)" button in the bottom navigation, which uses a more pronounced shadow to signify its role as the primary system entry point.

## Components

### Card de Aula (Class Card)
The flagship component. It must feature a white background and a 4px vertical status bar on the far left. The student's name is rendered in **Inter 500**, while the time and price are rendered in **IBM Plex Mono** for data precision.

### Navigation (Bottom Bar)
A fixed 5-item navigation bar. The central "Add" button is elevated and colored in `--purple-600`. Icons should be line-based and use `--purple-600` for active states and `--text-muted` for inactive states.

### Buttons & Chips
- **Primary Button:** Solid `--purple-600` with white text.
- **Secondary Button:** Light `--purple-100` background with `--purple-900` text.
- **Status Chips:** Small, rounded badges using the `--status` palette with a 10% opacity background of the same color for high legibility.

### Inputs
Text fields should use the `--bg-subtle` color for the fill with a subtle 1px border. Labels must be clear and use **Inter 500** for accessibility.
